How to test the scanner with a physical book
1. Print this page at 100% scale (Ctrl+P), then cut out each box and stick it on the back of any real book, or just keep the paper.
2. Run the two SQL files (schema_update_isbn.sql then sample_books.sql) so these test books exist in the system.
3. Sign in as a librarian, open Borrow, click Start camera scanner and hold a barcode about 20 cm from the camera. The ISBN fills in. Enter learner STU-2026-901 and confirm.
4. Open Return and scan the same barcode to bring it back.
Any real book works too: its back-cover barcode is the same EAN-13 type. Add its ISBN on the Books page first.
